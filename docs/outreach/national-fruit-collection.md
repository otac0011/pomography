**Subject:** Flavour and fruit-quality records for heritage apples - a request from a free apple atlas

Dear National Fruit Collection team,

I run Pomography (https://otac0011.github.io/pomography/), a free, non-commercial website that helps gardeners choose
heritage apples: taste, growing habit, rootstocks, pollination, and a world map that scores how each variety would
grow wherever you click, from real weather data.

We are working to make the flavour descriptions accurate and traceable. For every flavour note we now show which
independent sources support it, including 19th- and early-20th-century pomologies (Hogg, Bunyard, the Herefordshire
Pomona) and, where we can find them, measured sugar (Brix) and acidity values.

I would be grateful to know:

1. whether the Collection's variety descriptions or tasting-panel records (flavour notes, sweetness/acidity scores,
   Brix or titratable acidity, eating periods) are available for reuse, and on what terms, and
2. whether there is a preferred way to cite or link the Collection's pages for each variety.

We would credit the Collection on every page that uses its information and link back to it. I would also be happy
to share our list of varieties where published sources disagree about flavour, in case that is useful to you.

Many thanks for your time, and for looking after the collection.
