**Subject:** Permission to cite your apple tasting notes on a free heirloom-apple atlas

Dear Karim (and the Keepers team),

I run Pomography (https://otac0011.github.io/pomography/), a free, non-commercial website about heirloom apples. It
describes about 425 varieties - taste, growing habit, rootstocks, pollination - and has a world map that scores how
each apple would grow wherever you click, using real weather data. Your catalogue was the starting point: the site
covers the whole Keepers apple range and links every variety back to your page to buy.

Your copyright statement asks that your descriptions are not copied, so at the moment the site uses only the
variety names from your public category pages, and every description is our own. We are now working hard on making
the flavour descriptions accurate: each flavour note (rose-water, aniseed, pear-drop, nutty ...) is shown with the
independent sources that support it, including Hogg, Bunyard and other public-domain pomologies.

Your tasting notes are among the best there are, especially for the rarer varieties and for your own seedlings. Would
you allow us to:

1. record which flavour notes your descriptions give each variety (as short tags such as "aniseed" or "nutty", with
   "Keepers Nursery" named as the source and a link to your page), and
2. optionally quote up to one short sentence of your tasting note per variety, credited and linked?

We would not reproduce your full descriptions or any other part of your database, and we would remove anything you
ask us to at any time. If you would rather we only did (1), or neither, that is completely fine.

If it would help, I am also happy to send you the list of places where our sources disagree about a variety's
flavour - it might be useful for your own catalogue.
